Scoped potential EE481 project for upcoming semester - Hardware Accelerated Modulation Classifier for Real Time Spectrum Sensing - a CNN based AMC with FPGA accelerated interface. Split into two tracks: Python/PyTorch model side, and SystemVerilog RTL side.

Ran pre semester feasibility test on quantisation risk. Trained small CNN, 2 Conv and 2 FC Layers on RadioML 2016.10a, 11 Modulation types. Float32 baseline: 41.03%, static int8 quantisation (conv + linear) dropped accuracy by only 0.62pp.

Reran at 50 epochs for better trained baseline. Float32 improved to 48.20% static quantisation grew to 2.42pp.

Conclusion quantisation is not projects failure point.
